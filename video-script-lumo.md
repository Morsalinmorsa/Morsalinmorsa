# "The Lighthouse That Was Lonely" — Short Video Script

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 · ~90 seconds · blocky 3D "Roblox-style" animation
**Style:** Warm narrator voice (storybook tone), word-by-word bold white captions centered on screen, soft piano + gentle orchestral swell
**Characters:**
- **Mina** — 8-year-old girl, yellow raincoat, red boots, two short braids
- **Dad** — fisherman, beard, blue knit cap, orange rubber overalls
- **Lumo** — the giant lighthouse; red-and-white stripes, a big round glass "eye" at the top that can blink, glow and cry

---

## SCENE 1 — The town (0:00–0:08)
**Visual:** Sunny blocky seaside town. Colorful houses, small harbor with fishing boats, waves. Tall red-and-white lighthouse on a rocky cliff above it all. Slow push-in toward the lighthouse.
**Narration:** "In a little town by the sea, there's a lighthouse that nobody ever looks at."

## SCENE 2 — Nobody notices (0:08–0:15)
**Visual:** Townspeople walking past the base of the lighthouse, staring at phones, carrying shopping bags. Nobody looks up. Camera tilts up the tower — the lamp at the top is dark.
**Narration:** "Ships stopped coming years ago. So the people forgot it was even there."

## SCENE 3 — Mina (0:15–0:22)
**Visual:** Mina in her yellow raincoat skipping along the cliff path, holding a lunchbox. She stops at the lighthouse door and looks up curiously.
**Narration:** "Everyone… except Mina."

## SCENE 4 — The blink (0:22–0:30)
**Visual:** Close-up of the lamp glass at the top. Dust on it. Mina waves. The glass slowly *blinks* like an eye. Mina freezes, mouth open.
**Narration:** "One day she waved hello. And the lighthouse… blinked back."

## SCENE 5 — Lumo is alive (0:30–0:38)
**Visual:** The whole lighthouse gently leans down toward her, bricks creaking, the glass eye glowing soft and shy. Mina laughs and holds up her sandwich to share.
**Narration:** "His name was Lumo. And he hadn't had a friend in a very, very long time."

## SCENE 6 — Friendship montage (0:38–0:48)
**Visual (quick cuts):**
1. Mina sitting on the cliff reading a picture book aloud; Lumo's eye glows warmer.
2. Mina polishing the glass with a cloth; it sparkles.
3. Sunset — Mina asleep against the tower wall, Lumo shining a tiny light like a nightlight over her.
**Narration:** "Every day she came back. She read him stories. She cleaned his eye. And every night, he glowed a little brighter."

## SCENE 7 — The storm (0:48–0:58)
**Visual:** Night. Huge dark storm clouds, lightning, giant waves. In the harbor, Dad's small orange fishing boat is far out at sea, tossed around. Inside her house, Mina presses her face to the rainy window, scared.
**Narration:** "Then came the biggest storm the town had ever seen. And Mina's dad… was still out at sea."

## SCENE 8 — Lost at sea (0:58–1:04)
**Visual:** Dad on the boat, soaked, gripping the wheel, squinting into total darkness. No lights anywhere. Close-up of his worried face.
**Narration:** "It was so dark, he couldn't find his way home."

## SCENE 9 — Mina runs (1:04–1:10)
**Visual:** Mina running up the cliff path in the rain, raincoat flapping, lightning flashing behind her. She reaches Lumo and shouts upward.
**Mina (shouting):** "Lumo! Please — you have to shine!"

## SCENE 10 — Lumo shines (1:10–1:20)
**Visual:** Lumo's eye squeezes shut, trembling… then opens WIDE. A massive golden beam bursts out across the ocean, cutting through the rain. The beam sweeps over the waves and lands right on Dad's boat. Townspeople step out of their houses and look up at the lighthouse in amazement.
**Narration:** "And for the first time in years… the lighthouse shined with everything he had."

## SCENE 11 — Home (1:20–1:26)
**Visual:** Dad steers along the beam into the harbor. Morning-blue sky as the storm clears. Mina runs down the dock and jumps into his arms. Hug.
**Dad:** "I saw the light, kiddo. I followed it home."

## SCENE 12 — Ending (1:26–1:32)
**Visual:** Sunny morning. The whole town gathered on the cliff, kids decorating Lumo with flowers and a hand-painted sign: "THANK YOU LUMO." Lumo's eye glows happy, a single sparkly tear rolls down the glass. Final wide shot, slow pull-back.
**Narration:** "Now, nobody forgets the lighthouse. Because sometimes… the ones we stop noticing are the ones who bring us home."

**End card:** Title — *"The Lighthouse That Was Lonely"* · "Follow for more stories 💛"

---

## Production notes
- **Captions:** One word at a time, bold white with black outline, center of screen (like the reference video). Highlight key words in color: *blinked*, *Lumo*, *storm*, *shine*, *home*.
- **Music:** Soft piano (Scenes 1–6) → tense drums + rain (7–9) → big orchestral swell (10) → warm piano ending (11–12).
- **SFX:** Seagulls, waves, creaking bricks when Lumo moves, thunder, a soft "ding" when Lumo blinks.
- **Consistency:** Keep Mina, Dad and Lumo's designs identical in every shot (generate one character reference image first and reuse it).
